FAQ

Questions Answered.

What exactly are "50 variations" from one shoot? ▼
From a single shoot, we create different cuts, hook openings, lengths (6s, 15s, 30s, 60s), formats. One video becomes 4–5 separate testable ads. 12 base creatives × 4–5 variations = 50+ deliverables ready to upload directly to Meta Ads Manager.
Why not just use ChatGPT to write scripts? ▼
ChatGPT writes structure. It doesn't understand your specific buyer's insecurities, purchase triggers, or what makes them stop scrolling at 11pm. Every script we write starts with buyer psychology — what they fear, what they desire, what embarrasses them. That's why our UGC feels real and converts.
We've tried creators before and ROAS was 0.5x. Why would this be different? ▼
Because you were testing creators, not angles. Giving 10 creators the same script = 10 versions of the same failed ad. We test different emotional angles, hooks, and formats until we find what resonates. That's systematic. What you did before was hope.
How long before we see results? ▼
Most brands see improved thumbstop and hold rate in week 2. ROAS improvement depends on your ad spend and how quickly Meta's algorithm learns. Typically brands see meaningful ROAS improvement by week 4–6.
Do you manage Meta Ads too or just create content? ▼
Our core offering is content creation + variation engineering. Meta Ads Management is available as an add-on. We can also train your media buyer on how to structure testing using our deliverables.
We're not a D2C brand — can you still help? ▼
Yes. The principles apply to any brand running Meta ads — restaurants, real estate, clinics, SaaS, personal brands. If you're spending on ads and not testing enough creative volume — we can help.
What are your packages and pricing? ▼
We customise every engagement based on your brand's needs, current ad spend, and goals. Book a free 30-minute discovery call and we'll walk you through what makes sense for your brand specifically. No packages on the website — we don't believe in one-size-fits-all.
